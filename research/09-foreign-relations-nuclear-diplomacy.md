# 9. Iran in International Relations: Diplomacy, Sanctions, and Nuclear Issues

Study Iran's relations with neighboring states and global powers, the 1979 hostage crisis, Iran–Iraq War, sanctions, regional policy, nuclear diplomacy, the 2015 Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action (JCPOA), and later disputes through dated records and multiple perspectives.

The JCPOA was concluded in 2015 by Iran and the E3/EU+3, with UN Security Council Resolution 2231 endorsing the agreement. The Security Council's official background page records a change in the status of earlier resolutions effective in September 2025. Because legal and diplomatic developments are time-sensitive, verify current official records before publication.

Sources: https://main.un.org/securitycouncil/en/content/2231/background ; https://ola.iaea.org/Applications/FactSheets/Country/Detail?code=IR ; https://treaties.un.org/

Questions: What do treaty texts require and not require? What has the IAEA reported directly, and what is a political actor's characterization? How have sanctions affected state revenue, trade, services, and households? How do sovereignty, security, alliances, and domestic politics shape competing arguments?

**Caution:** Do not infer legal status from a headline or political speech. Record the exact document, provision, date, and status as of research.
