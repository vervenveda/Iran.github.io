# 10. Iran Today: Institutions, Society, and Open Questions

This is a dated research file, not a timeless summary. Political and economic facts change; claims about protests, casualties, arrests, elections, sanctions, negotiations, or public opinion require dates, careful attribution, and corroboration.

Separate the Iranian state and its institutions; officeholders and factions; residents with diverse views and experiences; diaspora communities; foreign governments; and independent researchers, journalists, and rights organizations.

For each current claim, ask: What exactly happened and when? Is there a primary record, official statement, dataset, court document, or independently verified report? What does the source know directly? Are figures confirmed or estimates? Are independent sources genuinely independent? What remains unknown? When was the page last updated?

Recurring topics: constitutional powers; women's rights, education, work, and public participation; ethnic and religious minorities; inflation, employment, energy, water, agriculture, and environment; internet access and censorship; migration and diaspora; sanctions and welfare; regional diplomacy and international law.
