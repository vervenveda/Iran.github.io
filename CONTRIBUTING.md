# Contributing

Help improve accuracy, traceability, clarity, and coverage.

For each proposed change, provide:
- the exact claim or passage;
- proposed revision;
- primary source(s), with stable links and page/line references;
- scholarly corroboration;
- counter-evidence or limitations;
- confidence assessment.

Avoid unsourced slogans, claims about entire ethnic or religious groups, treating official statements as independent verification, projecting the modern state onto ancient history, and copying copyrighted work without permission. Preserve correction history and identify uncertainty honestly.
