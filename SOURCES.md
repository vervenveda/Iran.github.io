# Selected Sources and Research Gateways

A starting bibliography, not an exhaustive list. Cite the exact item and passage used rather than only a general landing page.

## Scholarly reference works
- Encyclopaedia Iranica, “Iran”: https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/iran/
- Encyclopaedia Iranica, “Iranian History: Pre-Islamic Times”: https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/iran-ii1-pre-islamic-times/
- Encyclopaedia Iranica, chronology: https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/chronology-of-iranian-history-part-1/
- Cambridge History of Iran: https://www.cambridge.org/core/what-we-publish/collections/cambridge-histories
- Library of Congress, *Iran: A Country Study* (older survey; corroborate with newer research): https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/frd/frdcstdy/ir/irancountrystudy00curt_0/irancountrystudy00curt_0.pdf

## Ancient history and primary evidence
- Oxford, Behistun inscription: https://www.cabinet.ox.ac.uk/behistun-relief-and-inscription-0
- Oxford, Cyrus Cylinder: https://www.cabinet.ox.ac.uk/cyrus-cylinder-babylon-bm-0
- Metropolitan Museum, Cyrus Cylinder and Ancient Persia: https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2013/cyrus-cylinder
- UNESCO World Heritage, Iran: https://whc.unesco.org/en/statesparties/ir

## Modern history and documents
- Iran Data Portal, constitutional texts and debates: https://irandataportal.syr.edu/constitutions-and-constitutional-debates/
- U.S. Department of State, FRUS Iran 1951–1954: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1951-54Iran
- National Security Archive, 1953 coup records: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/iran/2017-06-15/iran-1953-state-department-finally-releases-updated-official-history

## International institutions
- IAEA Iran country information: https://ola.iaea.org/Applications/FactSheets/Country/Detail?code=IR
- UN Security Council, Resolution 2231 background: https://main.un.org/securitycouncil/en/content/2231/background
- UN Treaty Collection: https://treaties.un.org/
- OHCHR: https://www.ohchr.org/
- UNESCO World Heritage Convention: https://whc.unesco.org/

Institutional records and current status pages can change. Record publication and “last checked” dates for current affairs.
