# Primary Sources: Iran

## Ancient and pre-Islamic
- **Behistun inscription of Darius I:** trilingual Old Persian, Elamite, and Babylonian royal inscription. Primary evidence for royal ideology and Darius's official account of legitimacy; corroborate its narrative. https://www.cabinet.ox.ac.uk/behistun-relief-and-inscription-0
- **Cyrus Cylinder:** Babylonian-language royal inscription associated with Cyrus II's capture of Babylon in 539 BCE. Read within Babylonian royal and temple traditions; avoid equating it without qualification to a modern human-rights declaration. https://www.cabinet.ox.ac.uk/cyrus-cylinder-babylon-bm-0
- **Persepolis administrative tablets:** important for labor, ration distribution, administration, and movement; consult specialist editions.

## Modern documents
- **Fundamental Laws of 1906 and Supplementary Fundamental Laws of 1907:** https://irandataportal.syr.edu/constitutions-and-constitutional-debates/
- **Constitution of the Islamic Republic (1979; amended 1989):** Persian and English texts via the Iran Data Portal.
- **FRUS Iran 1951–1954:** diplomatic and declassified documentary collection covering the Mosaddeq period and Operation TPAJAX. https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1951-54Iran
- **UN Security Council Resolution 2231:** formal record for the JCPOA framework. https://main.un.org/securitycouncil/en/content/2231/background

An index is not itself proof. Record the exact document, edition, translation, date, and passage used.
