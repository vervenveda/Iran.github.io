# Iran Historical Repository

A source-linked historical research archive on Iran, its peoples, political institutions, languages, religions, cultural heritage, and place in regional and world history.

## Editorial purpose
This repository examines Iran across long time spans without treating a civilization, geographic region, people, dynasty, or modern state as one unchanged entity. It includes evidence that complicates national myths as well as evidence that supports historical continuity.

## Scope
Early societies and Elam; Media and Achaemenids; Hellenistic, Parthian, and Sasanian Iran; Islamic-era societies; Mongol and Timurid periods; Safavid and Qajar Iran; the Constitutional Revolution; Pahlavi rule, oil nationalization, and the 1953 coup; the 1979 Revolution and Islamic Republic; culture and communities; foreign relations and nuclear diplomacy; contemporary society and human rights.

## Method
Prefer original documents and artifacts, reliable editions and translations, archival records, archaeological publications, peer-reviewed scholarship, and reputable reference works. Official sources are essential for establishing what an institution says or has formally done, but are not automatically independent verification of every factual assertion they contain.

Labels used in this project:
- **Documented:** supported directly by a reliable record or strong converging evidence.
- **Well-supported:** multiple credible sources support the claim.
- **Contested:** significant evidence or interpretations differ.
- **Provisional:** plausible but needs more corroboration.
- **Unverified:** evidence is currently insufficient.
- **Corrected:** wording was revised; preserve the change log.

## Historical caution
“Iran,” “Persia,” “the Iranian plateau,” “Persian-speaking,” and “Iranian” are related but not interchangeable terms. Political borders, populations, languages, religions, and institutions changed over time. Do not project the modern nation-state unchanged onto ancient empires. Iran's communities are diverse; ethnicity, language, religion, citizenship, and political opinion do not always map neatly onto one another.

## Publish with GitHub Pages
Upload the contents of this folder to your repository root. In GitHub, open **Settings → Pages**, choose publishing from the `main` branch and root folder, and verify that `index.html` is in the published root.

The landing page is connected to the Verve N Veda ecosystem through the 333 Network ticker, an accessible ecosystem menu, the central World & Diplomacy directory, and a Free Khaemenes Academy learning pathway. The gateway and its five cultural portals share a persistent English ↔ Persian interface layer with right-to-left layout support.

This is a substantive starter archive, not a completed scholarly encyclopedia. Expand chapters through item-level research, exact citations, quotation checking, page references, and independent corroboration. Contemporary claims require dates and regular review.
