# Disputed Claims Register

This file identifies recurring questions for research; it does not decide them in advance.

- **Iran and Persia:** How did these terms change across geography, language, culture, and political rule?
- **Ancient continuity:** Which claims of continuity are supported by artifacts, inscriptions, language, or institutions, and which rely on later national histories?
- **Cyrus Cylinder:** The cylinder is a Babylonian royal inscription associated with Cyrus II's victory in Babylon. Modern comparisons with universal human-rights charters are interpretations and should be labelled as such.
- **Conquest and Islamization:** Political conquest, conversion, language change, and cultural transformation occurred unevenly over generations.
- **Safavid Iran:** Promotion of Twelver Shi'ism was a major transformation, but local implementation varied.
- **Constitutional Revolution:** Examine constitutionalist, royalist, clerical, merchant, labor, regional, and foreign perspectives.
- **1953 coup:** Use declassified U.S. records, British archival material where available, Iranian scholarship, and independent historians. Separate documented foreign involvement from debates about domestic actors and long-term consequences.
- **1979 Revolution:** Participants had diverse aims; do not retroactively treat all of them as supporters of the institutions that later consolidated power.
- **Contemporary Iran:** Distinguish state institutions, political factions, residents, diaspora communities, and foreign governments. Date all current claims.
