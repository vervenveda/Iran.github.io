Script

Persian writing is both a language technology and a visual art. Modern Persian uses an Arabo-Persian script, while calligraphic traditions such as nastaʿlīq gave poetry and manuscripts a distinctive visual rhythm.

01 / WRITING SYSTEM

Persian on the page

The modern Persian writing system developed through adaptation rather than simple copying.

Arabo-Persian script

Encyclopaedia Iranica describes modern Persian as written in the Arabo-Persian script.

Persian additions

Persian added letters for sounds not represented in the Arabic alphabet, including p, č, ž and g.

Right to left

Persian is written right to left, and most letters connect within words, giving typography a strongly flowing structure.

02 / CALLIGRAPHY

Writing becomes composition

Calligraphy turns language into measured visual form.

Nastaʿlīq

Nastaʿlīq became especially associated with Persian poetry and literary manuscripts.

Safeguarding

UNESCO selected Iran's national calligraphy safeguarding programme for its Register of Good Safeguarding Practices in 2021.

Manuscript integration

Calligraphy can be combined with illumination, diagonal text blocks, borders, miniatures and bindings as one designed object.
